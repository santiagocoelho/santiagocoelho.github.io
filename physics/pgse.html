About this teaching prototype & further exploration

This is a fast visual explanation, not a research sequence simulator. Ideal rectangular PGSE gradients and instantaneous RF pulses are assumed. The displayed echo time is 10 + Δ + δ ms: a schematic with 5 ms margins, not a scanner prescription. Relaxation, imaging gradients, ramps, exchange and measurement noise are omitted.

The Gaussian echo uses the analytic expression with Dg = Dₓ cos²θ + Dᵧ sin²θ. Illustrative trajectories use 1,200 seeded walkers per environment, 0.1 ms integration steps, and midpoint phase accumulation. The restricted environment is a 2D disk with reflecting steps at the wall, not a 3D sphere. The restricted echo is a Monte Carlo magnitude estimate, with sampling variability of order 1/√1,200 ≈ 0.03; tiny signals should not be interpreted quantitatively. It is intentionally a small ensemble for responsive interaction. The same seed keeps comparisons stable.

Both populations start uniformly inside a disk. Gaussian spins then move freely, without wrapping; spins leaving the displayed window still contribute to the signal. Phases are shown in the toggling frame (RF sign absorbed into the effective gradient). The 180° pulse therefore does not create a visual phase jump. Playback replays the calculated paths at a teaching-friendly speed.
